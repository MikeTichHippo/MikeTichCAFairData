Marketing Analysis: how big the market is and how we reach it

Public market data + weekly deck + CAC model

The market isn't what limits us. Our reach is: we see only ~4–6% of California's FAIR and E&S shoppers.

About 1M FAIR and E&S policies in California (~$3.1B) renew every year. Our CA lead flow fell 75% during 2026 as spend dropped. The cheapest growth is re-shopping our own renewals, then buying back the reach.

Key takeaways
Each 1% of FAIR share ≈ $18M.That’s about 6.7K policies. California is already our biggest agency state (15.2K home binds, vs 2.0K for Texas).
Renewals are free reach.About 1,700 of our FAIR customers renew in the next 12 months. Re-shopping them costs an email and an agent call, not media.
Spend still buys binds at a reasonable CAC.The CAC model has CA CAC rising only from $147 to $202 as monthly CA spend doubles.
