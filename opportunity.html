Customer opportunity: our California FAIR customers

Hippo agency FAIR Plan placements, Jan 2025 – Oct 2026 · 3,554 policies · 3,120 customers

We already handle ~2,900 FAIR customer decisions a year, and almost all of them go to other carriers.

When FAIR renews, our customers move to Aegis or Bamboo. Hippo gets 1% or less. Putting a Hippo admitted quote in front of them at renewal turns that churn into Hippo premium.

Key takeaways
FAIR is a one-year bridge.80% replace FAIR at renewal. We already have the relationship and know when they renew; Hippo just isn’t one of the options.
These are mainstream homes.Median home: built 1990, 2,000 sq ft, $556K rebuild cost, 85% owner-occupied. That fits an admitted HO-3, not E&S.
It’s mostly Southern California.65% are in SoCal and 32% in the Inland Empire alone, which also has the lowest premiums ($942 median).
They buy on price.Median FAIR premium is $1,430. A competing quote needs to land around $1.3–1.9K to win.
HHIP isn’t writing in CA today.Bamboo quotes 72% of these customers; Hippo was in front of almost none of them. Opening CA puts a Hippo quote in the room.

Every 10% we capture is worth ~$400K in premium a year

What we’d do

1. Re-shop every FAIR renewal

Put a Hippo quote in front of each FAIR customer 60–75 days before expiry. That’s about 1,700 customers over the next 12 months.

2. Lead with SoCal

The Inland Empire and San Diego have the most customers and the lowest premiums, so an admitted price can win there most easily.

3. Add DIC and bundles

99% of these customers have no DIC wrap or auto policy with us. A single Hippo policy can replace FAIR + DIC.
