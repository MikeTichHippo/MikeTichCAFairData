California: a Hippo carrier offer for FAIR and E&S customers

Executive summary · Nov 2026

Proposal: expand HHIP into California on our existing admitted paper from Jan 1, writing FAIR and E&S customers in lower wildfire-risk homes.

We already place ~2,900 FAIR customers a year with other carriers, and two-thirds of them re-shop with us at renewal. Our own loss history says we should only write the lower-risk part: HHIP's historical CA book ran a 89% loss ratio in 2023–25, versus 57% for lower wildfire bands. On that selective basis, 2027 is a ~$2.7M premium book (range $0.9M–$5.4M) that roughly breaks even against keeping the commission, with upside if loss ratios keep improving.
