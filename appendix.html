B · Gator quote data (Q4)

Quote rate by carrier on CA home requests

Successful quotes / attempted requests, Jan 2025 – Nov 2026

Hippo in Gator: test-like volume, never bound

  • 252K CA requests attempted, 68% quoted, 0 binds.
  • Only 90 Hippo quotes appeared next to another carrier's quote, so the attempts may be a separate flow (most came in Q3 2025).
  • Hippo stops appearing after Jan 2026. Recent declines are technical ("More information is needed", "Authentication with carrier failed").
  • Consistent with HHIP not being live in CA: Hippo was attempted on 9 of 14,489 FAIR/E&S-bound requests and 1 of 158,754 HO-3 requests (Q6b); the attempts were mostly HO-5/HO-6.
